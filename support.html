About Aisle Aura

Aisle Aura is your smart grocery list companion that helps you organize your shopping with ease. Create lists, share with family and friends, and never forget an item again.

Key Features:

  • Create and manage multiple grocery lists
  • Share lists with family and friends
  • Real-time synchronization across devices
  • Intuitive and easy-to-use interface
  • Works on iPhone, iPad, and web

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a new grocery list?
Tap the "+" button on the home screen, enter a name for your list, and start adding items. It's that simple!
How do I share a list with others?
Open the list you want to share, tap the share icon, and enter the email address of the person you want to invite. They'll receive an invitation to access the list.
Can I use Aisle Aura offline?
Yes! Your lists are stored on your device and will sync automatically when you're back online.
How do I delete my account?
Go to your Profile (tap the user icon), scroll down to find "Delete Account", and follow the confirmation steps. Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone.
Is my data secure?
Absolutely. We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your information is never shared with third parties without your consent.
How do I reset my password?
On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot Password?" and enter your email address. You'll receive a password reset link via email.
Can I edit or delete items from a shared list?
Yes! All members of a shared list can add, edit, or remove items. Changes sync in real-time for everyone.

Getting Started

New to Aisle Aura? Here's how to get started:

Step 1: Create an Account

Sign up using your email address or Apple ID. Your account will sync across all your devices.

Step 2: Create Your First List

Tap the "+" button to create a new list. Give it a name like "Weekly Groceries" or "Weekend BBQ".

Step 3: Add Items

Tap on your list and start adding items. You can check them off as you shop!

Step 4: Share with Others

Invite family members or roommates to collaborate on your lists in real-time.

Troubleshooting

App won't sync?

Check your internet connection and make sure you're signed in. Try closing and reopening the app.

Can't see shared lists?

Make sure you've accepted the invitation in your email. Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

App crashes or freezes?

Try force-closing the app and restarting it. If the problem persists, contact us at the email above with details about what you were doing when it occurred.
